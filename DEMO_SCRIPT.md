# SCTI Portal — Demo Script

**Project:** Sindhuli Community Technical Institute (SCTI) Web Portal  
**Duration:** ~8–12 minutes  
**Audience:** Evaluators, stakeholders, or general viewers  

---

## Before You Start

- Make sure XAMPP/WAMP is running (Apache + MySQL)
- Database `scti_school` is imported and seeded
- Browser is open at `http://localhost/scti`
- Have credentials ready for all three roles (admin, teacher, student)
- Clear browser cache or use incognito mode for a clean session

---

## Scene 1 — Public Landing Page (0:00 – 1:00)

**What to show:**
1. Open `http://localhost/scti` in the browser
2. Point out the **header** with the SCTI logo and navigation menu
3. Scroll through the **hero banner** and scrolling notice ticker at the top
4. Click **Programs** in the nav — show the list of available programs (B.Tech IT, Diploma Civil, etc.)
5. Click **Gallery** — show the public photo gallery with categories
6. Click **Notice Board** — show public notices
7. Click **Contact Us** — show the contact form

**What to say:**
> "This is the public-facing homepage of the SCTI portal. Anyone can browse programs, view the gallery, read notices, and reach out through the contact form — no login required."

---

## Scene 2 — Admin Login & Dashboard (1:00 – 3:00)

**What to show:**
1. Click **Login** in the nav
2. Select role: **Admin**, enter admin credentials, click Login
3. Land on the **Admin Dashboard**
4. Point out the **statistics cards**: Total Students, Total Teachers, Active Programs, Notices, Gallery Images, Contact Messages
5. Scroll to the **Recent Activity Feed**
6. Click **Manage Students** — show the student list with search
7. Click **Add Student** — briefly show the auto-generated credentials (username, password, student ID)
8. Go back, click **Manage Teachers** — show teacher list
9. Click **Manage Programs** — show active programs with subject lists
10. Click **Manage Notices** — show notice list, click **Add Notice**, show audience targeting (All / Students / Teachers / Emergency)
11. Click **Manage Gallery** — show image upload and categories
12. Click **View Contact Messages** — show submitted contact forms

**What to say:**
> "The admin has full control over the institution. From one dashboard they can manage students, teachers, programs, notices, and the gallery — and see everything happening in real time."

---

## Scene 3 — Teacher Login & Features (3:00 – 6:30)

**What to show:**
1. Log out, go back to Login
2. Select role: **Teacher**, enter teacher credentials, click Login
3. Land on the **Teacher Dashboard**
4. Point out stats: Total Students, My Assignments, Course Materials, Ungraded Submissions (with badge)
5. Click **Mark Attendance**
   - Select a class/subject and date
   - Mark a few students present, absent, late
   - Add a late reason for one student
   - Save attendance
6. Click **Attendance Charts** — show the visual analytics
7. Click **Assignments**
   - Show the assignment list
   - Click **Create Assignment** — fill in title, description, due date, class
   - Save it
8. Click **View Submissions** on an existing assignment — show student submissions
9. Click **Grade** on a submission — enter marks and save
10. Click **Enter Grades** — show the grade entry form (internal + external marks)
11. Click **Grade Charts** — show grade distribution visuals
12. Click **Course Materials** — show uploaded materials, click **Upload** to show the upload form
13. Click **My Profile** — show teacher profile page

**What to say:**
> "Teachers have everything they need to manage their classes. They can mark attendance, create and grade assignments, upload study materials, and track student performance — all from one place."

---

## Scene 4 — Student Login & Features (6:30 – 9:30)

**What to show:**
1. Log out, go back to Login
2. Select role: **Student**, enter student credentials, click Login
3. If first login — show the **forced password change** screen, set a new password
4. Land on the **Student Dashboard**
5. Point out stats: Enrolled Subjects, Attendance Rate (highlight warning if below 75%), Current GPA, Pending Assignments
6. Click **Attendance** — show attendance history and percentage
7. Click **My Grades** — show subject-wise grades (internal, external, total, grade letter)
8. Click **Assignments**
   - Show pending assignments with due dates
   - Click an assignment — show details
   - Click **Submit** — upload a file, submit
9. Click **Timetable** — show the class schedule
10. Click **Library** — show available course materials for download
11. Click **My Profile** — show profile, edit phone/address, save
12. Click **Change Password** — show the password change form

**What to say:**
> "Students get a clean, focused view of everything relevant to them — their attendance, grades, assignments, timetable, and study materials. They can submit assignments and manage their profile directly from the portal."

---

## Scene 5 — Notice Board & Remember Me (9:30 – 11:00)

**What to show:**
1. Log out, go to Login
2. Check the **Remember Me** checkbox, log in as student
3. Close the browser tab, reopen `http://localhost/scti` — show auto-login working
4. Navigate to **Notice Board** — show notices filtered by audience (General, Emergency highlighted)
5. Show an emergency notice with its distinct styling

**What to say:**
> "The Remember Me feature keeps users logged in securely for 30 days. The notice board filters notices by audience — students only see what's relevant to them, and emergency notices stand out immediately."

---

## Scene 6 — Wrap Up (11:00 – 12:00)

**What to say:**
> "The SCTI portal brings together everything a technical institute needs — student and teacher management, attendance, assignments, grading, course materials, notices, and a public-facing website — in one unified system. It's role-based, secure, and built to handle the day-to-day operations of the institution."

---

## Tips for Recording

- Use a browser zoom of **90%** so more content fits on screen
- Disable browser notifications before recording
- Use a mouse cursor highlighter tool (e.g., PowerToys, Cursor Highlight) to draw attention
- Record at **1920×1080** for best quality
- Narrate live or add voiceover in post-production
- Trim dead time between actions in editing
