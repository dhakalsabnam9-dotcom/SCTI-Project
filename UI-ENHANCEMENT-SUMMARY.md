## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
- **Primary Blue:** #004080
- **Secondary Blue:** #0059b3
- **Dark Blue:** #00264d
- **White:** #ffffff
- **Grey Background:** #f2f2f2
- **Text Dark:** #333
- **Text Light:** #666

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Arial, sans-serif
- **Headings:** Bold, colored #004080
- **Body Text:** Regular, #666

### Spacing
- **Section Padding:** 40px vertical
- **Card Padding:** 25-30px
- **Grid Gap:** 20-30px

### Effects
- **Box Shadow:** 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)
- **Hover Shadow:** 0 8px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)
- **Border Radius:** 10px
- **Transitions:** 0.3s ease
